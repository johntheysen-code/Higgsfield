## Why this result is unexpected and possibly misleading

Cultural signal says backyard chickens is a rising trend (post-2022 egg-price
boom, "crazy chicken lady" identity). The Amazon data does not back that.
Two hypotheses:

**(a) Wrong keyword.** `backyard chickens` may not be where buyers search.
Better candidates: `chicken keeper`, `chicken mom`, `crazy chicken lady`,
`homestead chicken`. Small sample (47 listings vs 90–190 norm) suggests
this — MerchFlux isn't returning a full picture for this phrase.

**(b) Off-Amazon market.** Chicken/homestead enthusiasts may buy more from
craft fairs, Etsy, farm-store channels than Amazon POD. The community is
real but not on this platform.

## What this niche tells us (even if we skip it)

- The recent winner is **humor** ("Chickens Make Me Happy...") not identity.
- Specific breed angles (Silkie, Polish, Buff Orpington) all underperform —
  same pattern as Chickadee/Owl in birding: popular species ≠ selling species.
- "Crazy Chicken Mama" / "Chicken Mom" identity DOES appear, but mid-tier BSR.

## Why it's a pass
Lots of **supply** (people mass-uploading template designs — "OTA Groovy/Boho/Squad"
near-duplicates) with almost no **demand** (N/A BSR = no sales rank = not selling).
The niche is being actively flooded right now by template sellers. Entering means
competing with daily uploads for ~zero sales.

## The key tell: N/A BSR
On Amazon, **N/A BSR = no sales rank = essentially not selling.** A *good* niche
CSV will have many listings with real BSRs (e.g. under ~300K). This one doesn't.
That single column is the clearest "avoid" signal here.

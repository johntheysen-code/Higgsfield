2. **Wiener-innuendo humor** — 33% of the niche, BSR 174K winner. "Proud to Have a Small Wiener" type — community embraces, Amazon-acceptable since "wiener" is the literal breed nickname.

## What to SKIP

- **"Friends in Low Places"** (2 listings selling, BSR 327K–423K) — **Garth Brooks song lyric, TM RISK.** Amazon may remove or seller may get DMCA. Skip despite the BSR.

## The two clear opportunities

### 1. "Just A Girl Who Loves Chickens" — floral aesthetic
- **BSR 427K**, recent **2026-04**, "Cute Floral" framing.
- **Mirrors our existing floral collection aesthetic exactly.**
- Could be made in Kittl with templates similar to our pickleball/crochet line.
- Low-friction adaptation of what already works for us.

### 2. "Rooster Wearing Sunglasses" — the cleanest standout

## What to SKIP

- **Egg dealer** — 13 attempts, median 1.47M. Saturated angle.
- **Grandma / Nana** — **0 listings in data.** Could be untapped gap, BUT given
  grandma's been dead in quilting / crochet / birding (3 niches confirmed),
  the pattern strongly suggests it's pickleball-only. **Don't test here.**

## Pattern confirmation: Father's Day works for outdoor/animal niches

Chicken Daddy (Father's Day) appears here, just like Bird Nerd Fathers Day in
birding. Pattern: Father's Day gift angle works for hobbies men actually do.
Mom angle is alive but less standout. Grandma works only for pickleball.

## Recommendation

**Pursue chicken keeper.** Two designs to brief next:

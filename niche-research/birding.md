## The two standout opportunities

### 1. "Bird Nerd × [specific species]" — modular winning format
- **"Bird Nerd Blue Jay"** — BSR **315K**, listed 2026-03-12, Average rating.
- **"Bird Nerd Cardinal"** — BSR 661K, listed 2025-11-24.
- 23 total "Bird Nerd" variants in the data = the formula is *proven and repeatable*.
- **Why this matters:** one slogan format spawns many designs by swapping the
  species — Blue Jay, Cardinal, Hummingbird, Goldfinch, Indigo Bunting, etc.
  Buyers self-select by their favorite bird. Volume play.

### 2. Minimalist line-art bird aesthetic
- "Minimal Line Art Minimalist Abstract Sketch Scribble Bird" — BSR **384K**,
  listed 2026-01-12. Single hit, but the top-performing aesthetic in the niche.
- **Why this matters:** the data says modern minimalist line-art beats vintage
  illustrated for birding. Differentiated style, less saturated, premium feel.

## Trademark

All generic (bird names, "bird nerd", "birding"). No risk. Avoid: Audubon
Society logo, specific birding apps (eBird, Merlin), state-bird gov references.

## What to SKIP

- **Vintage/retro birding** — 21 listings, median 2M. Over-saturated style.
- **Owl, Chickadee** — popular species but the data says dead. People want
  Cardinal, Blue Jay, Hummingbird instead.
- **Coffee, Grandma, Retired, "I watch birds"** — all median >2M = dead angles.
- **Generic "funny bird watcher"** — wall-to-wall, BSR mostly 700K+, hard to differentiate.

## Recommendation

**Pursue birding next.** Two angles to brief for Kittl:

1. **"Bird Nerd × [species]"** — start with the proven winners (Blue Jay, Cardinal,
   Hummingbird) — 3 designs from one format. Modular, repeatable, proven.
2. **Minimalist line-art bird** — single bold bird design, premium aesthetic.
   The top-performing visual direction in the niche.

Backup: **"How to Identify Birds" humor chart** if you want a 4th. Skip
vintage/owl/chickadee.

**Aesthetic shift:** birding buyers are more mixed-gender and Father's-Day gifty
than our 50+ women collection. The floral feminine look won't fit — recommend
nature-illustrated or clean minimalist instead. Different palette: deep greens,
warm earth tones, dusty blues (forest/sky palette, not warm cream/terracotta).

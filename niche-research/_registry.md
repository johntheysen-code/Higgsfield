| **WEAK** | <30 under 1M, lots of dead, no clean lane | Skip generation; consider only 1 niche-anchored test |
| ❌ **DEAD** | No real BSR pool, near-empty / fully saturated dead | Walk away |

## "Potential bestseller" — evaluation criteria

When a new niche CSV comes in, judge it against the registry like this. **All criteria together = the verdict.** Single signals don't override the whole picture.

### Strong-bias criteria (push toward GENERATE)

- [ ] **Has blockbusters** — at least 1–3 listings under 300K BSR
- [ ] **High under-1M density** — at least 50% of niche-specific listings selling under 1M
- [ ] **Low dead inventory** — under 20% of listings above 2M BSR
- [ ] **Clear standout angle** — either a high-quality crossover (X × Y pattern) or a low-comp lane (comp <150)
- [ ] **Specific identity hook** — buyer can self-identify ("I am a X")
- [ ] **Gift-friendly** — Father's Day / Mother's Day / birthday / occasion-driven
- [ ] **Aesthetic fits our capabilities** — floral / badge / minimalist / meme / mystical (we know these)
- [ ] **Color profile clear** — p4 OR p5 OR dual-variant trigger from style-indicator analysis

### Weak-bias criteria (push toward SKIP)

- [ ] **No blockbusters** — 0 listings under 300K
- [ ] **Low under-1M density** — fewer than 30 selling
- [ ] **High dead inventory** — over 50% above 2M (over-fished)
- [ ] **Saturated top lanes** — every recurring angle has comp >1000
- [ ] **TM trap dominance** — top sellers are mostly parodies (Hell's Kitchen, GoT, LOTR, X-Files, etc.)
- [ ] **No clear niche-anchor** — generic terms only, no specific identity
- [ ] **Wrong keyword pull** — small dataset suggests we're searching wrong term

### Override flags

- 🚨 **Single low-comp blockbuster** (comp <50, BSR <300K) is a STRONG override even in an otherwise saturated niche — that's a clean mirror opportunity
- 🚨 **Fresh-wave signal** (3+ listings from last 60 days all selling) = rising market, generate even in moderate niches
- 🚨 **Cross-niche pattern hit** (e.g., crossover sub-lane + Father's Day + breed-specific) = compound signal

### Verdict translation

```
Strong criteria: 6+ checked, weak criteria: 0–2  → 🔥 GENERATE 3+ designs
Strong criteria: 4–5, weak criteria: 0–3        → ⭐ GENERATE 2 careful designs
Strong criteria: 2–4, weak criteria: 3–5        → MODERATE — 1–2 micro-test
Strong criteria: 0–2, weak criteria: 5+          → SKIP / WALK AWAY
```

4. Apply the "Potential Bestseller" criteria checklist

6. **Append to this registry** under the master metrics table
7. Recommend: GENERATE (and what), SUB-SCAN (and what next), or SKIP

This file is the **comparison memory** — without it, every new niche scan starts from zero. With it, we know whether a new niche is in alien territory, sourdough territory, or somewhere new entirely.

## Honest recommendation

**Walk away from sourdough as a primary niche.** Three reasons:

1. **The portfolio is already at ~50 designs unvalidated.** Adding more from a
   weak niche dilutes the signal we'll get back from the strong niches.
2. **The headline numbers don't justify the effort.** 19 selling listings,
   4 Good ratings, 58% dead — these are quitting metrics for any niche scan.
3. **The strongest single signal is too crowded already** ("Don't Get Me
   Started" has 12 competitors; "Sourdough Dealer" has 20). We'd enter as
   competitor 13 or 21, fighting for table scraps.

**If we insist on testing it, ONE design only:**

- **Sourdough Tarot Card / Mystical Skeleton** — mirrors BSR 438K Good +
  comp 116 LOW (the niche's single cleanest signal). 2 competitors total in
  this sub-lane. Same framework that's worked for celestial designs in the
  golden retriever niche. Estimated upside: small steady earner, not a
  bestseller.

**My straight call:** **skip sourdough.** Walk back to the discipline:
- Ship the 50 designs in flight
- Wait for sales signal
- Pick the next niche based on what proves out

The pattern I have to confront: I've been wrong on the pre-scan recommendation

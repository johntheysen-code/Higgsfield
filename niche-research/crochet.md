## What works / what to skip

**Pursue:**
1. **Crochet × Dog** — the rising wave. 2–3 designs riding the "Crochet & My Dog"
   energy in our floral/magenta look. Get in while the angle is fresh.
2. **Crochet × Sheep** (backup) — single top seller at BSR 489K, comp 108. Cute,
   under-served.
3. **Sass humor** ("Less Murdery", "Punching People is Wrong") — proven sub-segment.

**Skip:**
- **Grandma/Nana** — DEAD (median BSR 2.9M). Second niche where grandma fails.
  Pattern emerging: grandma works for pickleball, dies for craft niches.
- **Cats** — 20 listings but median 2.1M = noisy/saturated, hard to differentiate.
- **Coffee, Era, Reading/Books, Therapy** — all dead-zone medians.

## Trademark

All angles generic (crochet, knit, dog, sheep, yarn). No risk. Avoid specific
brand names (Lion Brand, Red Heart) and pattern designer names.

## Recommendation

**Test crochet via the Dog angle.** 2–3 designs on the proven floral look,
magenta knockout, mirroring the rising "Crochet and Hang Out with My Dog"
energy. Cheaper to validate before stacking more. If they land, deepen with
sheep + sass. If they don't, the crochet niche moves to the back burner —
quilting becomes the secondary candidate.

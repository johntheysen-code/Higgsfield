## Why this niche
- **High affinity, evergreen.** Axolotls have a strong, durable fandom (cute
  animal + mild novelty). Buyers self-identify ("axolotl person") which is ideal
  for apparel.
- **Low trademark risk.** Generic real animal — no brand/character IP to infringe
  (avoid game-specific axolotl skins / named characters).

## The angle (avoid the saturated center)
- Saturated: plain "cute axolotl" illustrations, "axolotl" text tees.
- **Chosen angle:** a **pun + relatable-overwhelm** design — "A LOTL GOING ON"
  (a lot going on). Combines the cute-animal niche with the *funny / mental-health
  / busy-life humor* angle the source videos flagged as strong sellers. The
  pun widens the audience beyond pure axolotl fans (anyone feeling busy/stressed).

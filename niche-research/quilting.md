# Niche: Quilting — SCORED (MerchFlux data, 2026-05-30)

**Verdict: VIABLE but MODERATE — alive, less hot than pickleball.** Data:
`data/amazon/quilting__2026-05-30.csv` (141 listings, US, T-Shirt; 90 quilting-specific).

## Quilting vs Pickleball (honest comparison)

| Signal | Pickleball | **Quilting** (quilting-specific only) |
|---|---|---|
| Listings with real BSR | 100% (91/91) | 100% (141/141 in raw pull) |
| BSR <300K (blockbusters) | 4 | **0** |
| BSR 300–600K (strong) | 41 | **8** |
| BSR 600K–1M (steady) | 43 | **31** |
| Total under 1M (selling) | 88 | **39** |
| Opportunity "Good" rating | 20 | **3** |
| Read | hot — many designs really selling | alive, but slower velocity per design |

Quilting moves real volume but doesn't have pickleball's blockbusters. The
niche is fragmented — 51 of the 141 search results are off-niche cross-tags
(archery, biker, "I do my own stunts", etc.), so the *actual* quilting market
is the ~90 filtered listings, of which only ~8 are doing >300K BSR steady.

## The standout angle: Quilting × Cats (women's crossover)

Same pattern that worked in pickleball — a hobby crossover that targets a
specific overlap demographic.

- **"Fabricologist Quilting Crew I Never Quilt Alone I Have Cats"** —
  BSR 624K, comp **89 LOW**, "Good" rating, listed 2019. Long-durable seller.
- **"Cat Sewing If You See Me with A Seam Ripper"** — BSR 605K, comp 313.
- **"Oops I Bought More Fabric Cat Sewing Quilting"** — BSR 978K, recent 2026-04.
- **"Funny Cat Quilting Sewing Sayings I Need More Fabric Quilt"** — BSR 1.1M, 2025-08.
- **"I Never Quilt Alone I Have Cats Sewing Quilter Quilting"** — BSR 1.9M, 2024.

The "Cats + quilting" combo appears **5×** in the data with one strong seller
(comp 89 LOW = the clearest opportunity). Buyer = older woman with cats who
quilts. Exact demographic our floral aesthetic already serves.

## Other promising angles

- **"Quilting With My Peeps" (bird pun)** — BSR **364K, comp 70 LOW, "Good"**.
  The strongest single quilting-specific listing in the dataset. Two variants
  exist. Cute pun, gift-friendly.
- **"I'll Be in My Office Sewing" / "Heading to My Sewing Room"** — recent
  (2024–26), multiple variants, BSR 674–718K. Trending identity slogan.
- **"I Still Play with Blocks"** (pun on quilt blocks) — 2 listings, ~605–760K.
- **Faith / "Blessed are Piecemakers"** — multiple, slower-selling but a real
  sub-segment.
- **"Quilt Gang" / sassy sewing sisters** — 2 listings ~900K.

## What's NOT working (warning signs)

- **Grandma angle is dead here** — opposite of pickleball: "Quilting Nana Quilt
  Grandma" (BSR 1.3M Poor), "Cool Sewing Art for Women Grandma" (1.6M Poor),
  "Quilting Design for Women Grandma" (1.3M Poor). Three grandma listings, all
  underperforming. Skip this angle.
- **Generic "funny quilting"** — over-saturated, mostly Average/Poor.

## Trademark

All angles above use generic terms (quilting, sewing, fabric, cats, peeps).
No TM risk. Avoid specific brand names (Singer, Janome) and pattern designer
names.

## Recommendation

Quilting is **worth a small test**, not a heavy bet:
- **Sharpest angle:** **Quilting × Cats** ("I Never Quilt Alone I Have Cats" pattern).
  Mirrors the mahjong/pickleball crossover playbook. 2–3 designs on the proven
  floral look should validate cheaply.
- **Backup angle:** **"Quilting With My Peeps"** (the bird pun that's already winning).
- **Skip:** grandma (data shows it dies here), generic funny quilting (saturated).

Honestly: if you have another niche queued (crochet, birding) consider pulling
that one too and comparing — quilting is viable but not hot like pickleball.

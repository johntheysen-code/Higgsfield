`data/amazon/vacation-bible-scholl__2026-06-02.csv` (184 listings, 184

copyrighted VBS curriculum themes published by Christian publishers (Group

infringement risks** — even if they're selling, we can't safely mirror them.

## Buyer-market shape — DIFFERENT from our usual

**VBS is a CHURCH-GROUP-PURCHASE market.** Buyer is a coordinator buying
20–50 shirts for staff/kids at a summer program. This shapes everything:

- **Curriculum themes win** because churches order matching shirts for their
  theme — this is why TM'd shirts dominate
- **Generic VBS shirts are weaker** because churches prefer theme-coordinated
- **One-off individual identity buyers are tiny** — different conversion
  dynamics than our usual portfolio
- **Highly seasonal** — June-August buying window only
- **Annual rotation required** — "VBS 2026" obsoletes "VBS 2025" overnight

This is **not** the same kind of POD market as our pet/hobby/identity niches.

## Cross-niche pattern checks

- ✅ **Grandma absent** — pattern holds (9 niches confirmed)
- ❌ **"In My X Era" doesn't fit** — not religious-appropriate
- ❌ **Minimalist line art absent** — kids/church program shirts want energetic visuals
- ❌ **Identity/role humor absent** — religious context, no fake-credential humor
- ⚠️ **Year-tagged designs require annual rotation** — VBS 2026 → VBS 2027 → ... ongoing maintenance
- ⚠️ **New pattern emerging:** **"Curriculum-driven niches"** are TM-trap categories. Watch for similar shapes in homeschool / Sunday school / specific programs.

## Shirt-color analysis (Step 3.5d)

- Light shirts dominant (kids' shirts often white/bright)
- p5 dominant; no real p4 dual signal

2. **`christian summer camp`** — broader camp market (not VBS-specific theme)
3. **`y'all southern christian`** — Southern Christian identity (saw BSR 386K Good)
4. **`bible study group`** — adult Bible study has its own market

## Recommendation

**SKIP this niche.** Three converging reasons:

1. **TM trap density** — the top BSR signals are curriculum themes we can't
   safely mirror. Going generic puts us in a smaller saturated lane.
2. **Wrong buyer-market shape** — church group purchase = not our individual
   identity/gift buyer. Lower-fit with our existing portfolio approach.
3. **Annual rotation overhead** — VBS 2026 designs obsolete in 12 months.
   Maintenance cost without recurring revenue (since church buys once).

**If you genuinely want to test Christian/religious POD,** scan `sunday school
teacher` or `christian summer camp` separately — those have similar buyer
energy but more individual-buyer share and less curriculum-TM exposure.

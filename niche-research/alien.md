## What to SKIP

- ⚠️ **"Rocky The Alien" (BSR 23K, 33K, 280K, 316K)** — DO NOT MIRROR. Multiple
  listings with the same character name suggest it's a specific named character
  (likely viral TikTok/internet meme character). Unknown TM status, high risk.
  The fact that 6 different titles use the exact name "Rocky the Alien" + the
  high BSRs + multiple sellers racing = looks like an IP grab.
- ⚠️ **"Spurs Fox Alien Castle Harper" (7 listings, BSRs 97K–311K)** — KEYWORD
  SPAM. The brand name "Spurs Fox Alien Castle Harper" + 7 near-identical
  titles = a seller stuffing unrelated keywords into the title. Not a real
  angle to mirror.
- ❌ **"I Want to Believe"** (BSR 332K) — **X-Files TM.** Skip.
- ❌ **Generic "Green Alien Head"** (BSR 297K, comp **2000**) — saturated.
- ❌ **Specific franchise references** — Alien movie, Predator, E.T., Marvel
  Guardians of the Galaxy, Ben 10, etc. All TM.

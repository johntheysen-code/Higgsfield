## Shirt-color analysis (Step 3.5d)

Style indicators in under-1M pool:
- **Vintage / retro / 90s / 70s**: ~12% (p4 dark)
- **Floral / wildflower (retro wedding mom)**: ~5% (p5 light)
- **Western / cowboy**: ~5% (p4 typically — chambray, denim, vintage)
- **Minimal/simple**: ~5% (p5 light)
- **Chaos/feral/madness**: ~12% (p4 dark — works on black for the chaos vibe)

**Total p4 signal: ~30%. Total p5 signal: ~10%.**

**Recommendation:** **p5 default** (white shirts dominant for bachelorette
parties — bride often wears white anyway), with **p4 dark for the feral/madness
aesthetic specifically**. Just below the 20% dual-variant threshold for most
designs, but the Feral Bride lane wants p4 explicitly.
